The accessions here and metadata are associated with the paper, "Emergence and interstate spread of highly pathogenic avian influenza A(H5N1) in dairy cattle in the United States" (https://doi-org.stanford.idm.oclc.org/10.1126/science.adq0900).

Raw sequencing data generated from the paper are at the Sequencing Read Archive (SRA) and listed in their table S1.

Samples we are using are a subset from Bioproject: PRJNA1102327.
